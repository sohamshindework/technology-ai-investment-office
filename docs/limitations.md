# Current limitations

The quarterly rollout/valuation inconsistency is resolved: the selected cohorts drive costs, benefits, resource use, the complete roadmap and tracking. This remains a local analytical product, not a production approval system.

1. **Fixed phasing:** the optimizer chooses among discrete scales with deterministic quarterly phasing. It does not optimize arbitrary start dates or cohort placement. A different phasing policy could use spare late-quarter capacity and improve value. OPTIMAL means optimal under the implemented policy and integer precision, not all possible schedules.
2. **Hypothetical economics:** all Supply Chain investment budgets, resource limits, costs, benefits and probabilities are assumptions. There is no cost calibration, benefit-overlap adjustment, tax, working-capital, terminal-value or failure-contagion model. Include implementation wages in input costs; do not double-count them.
3. **Expected cost limits:** resource constraints use expected sampled capex, not a guarantee against stochastic cost overruns. Midpoint quarterly benefit ramps and whole-quarter rounded deployment/gate delays are approximations. Shared demand is the only cross-initiative outcome correlation.
4. **Governance:** planned gates consume model time and capacity but do not certify legal approval. Default assessments remain pending human review. The bounded questionnaire does not cover every sector, prohibited practice, role or exception.
5. **Benefits:** observed benefits are unavailable by default. The optional fictional dataset is explicitly labeled and never presented as company performance. CSV imports validate identity, period, currency, structure and declared provenance, not whether an observation is true or causally attributable to an initiative. Variance uses only matching observed periods; missing periods remain unavailable. The workflow does not perform counterfactual attribution, automated source verification, FX conversion or operational KPI/milestone ingestion.
6. **Scope:** the flagship logistics case is DHL Supply Chain only. DHL Group reports supply divisional facts; there is no Group-wide investment allocation or recommendation. Allianz and BMW Group are separate illustrative cases with their own assumptions and evidence. The reusable engine is company-agnostic.
7. **Research/exports:** research extraction is deterministic, English-language and keyword-based. It does not perform OCR, retrieve URLs, infer unsupported claims or estimate economics. The small reviewed set measures bounded fixtures, not general accuracy. Scanned/encrypted PDFs are rejected. There is no research LLM. Excel and PDF exports are presentation snapshots of the current calculated analysis; workbook cells are not a second editable financial model, and the documents do not include uploaded research content or constitute approval.
8. **Operations:** one local user, SQLite snapshots, self-reported reviewers, no authenticated approvals, RBAC, encryption at rest or production monitoring. The review trail is not tamper-proof. Do not publish local databases or user-supplied actuals.
9. **Reproducibility:** exact version pins and local tests are provided, without artifact hashes, formal type checking, remote CI success or cross-platform deployment certification.

Priority improvements are flexible time-indexed scheduling if business use requires it, calibrated assumptions and benefits overlap, operational KPI imports, broader document evaluation or optional model assistance, and production access controls. They are separate from the completed consistency correction.
